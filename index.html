The short version

I'm a non‑traditional HR person.

Most HR problems aren't people problems — they're design problems wearing a people costume. A rating that feels unfair. A manager who avoids the hard conversation. A high performer who leaves because nobody showed them the next step.

I'm a relentlessly curious learner. When I hit a broken process, I don't write a policy about it — I go learn the tool, prototype the fix, and put something in a leader's hands by Friday. Pairing AI and structured problem-solving with real HR judgement is how you get customised solutions that businesses actually adopt — and how you get numbers to show for it.

01 Live app

HRBP Coach

A 1:1 coaching guide for managers

Managers are the single biggest lever on retention and engagement — and almost none of them are taught how to actually run a 1:1. HRBP Coach turns a vague "catch-up" into a structured coaching conversation: scenario-based prep, the right questions for the moment (under-performance, retention risk, growth, feedback that landed badly), and follow-through so the next conversation builds on the last.

  • Built forPeople managers & HRBPs
  • SolvesUnstructured, avoided or repetitive 1:1s
  • GoalCut 1:1 prep from ~30 min to under 5

02 Live app

Rating Reflex

Performance rating assistance for team leaders

Rating season is where good intentions meet recency bias. Rating Reflex helps a team leader turn scattered observations and feedback into a defensible rating — mapped against level-wise expectations, checked for biased or vague language, and pressure-tested before it ever reaches calibration. Fewer surprises in the room, fairer pay-for-performance outcomes out of it.

  • Built forTeam leaders & reviewers
  • SolvesBias, skew and "I can't justify this rating"
  • Kin toThe AI Rating Recommender I shipped at Deloitte

03 Live app

Career Mobility

Internal movement, made visible

People don't leave for money as often as they leave because they can't see a next step. This one maps what someone already knows onto the roles adjacent to them — surfacing realistic internal moves, the gap between here and there, and what closes it. Retention as a navigation problem instead of a counter-offer problem.

  • Built forEmployees, HRBPs & talent teams
  • SolvesInvisible internal opportunity → avoidable attrition
  • GoalTurn exit conversations into mobility ones
